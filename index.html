Questions

What does a quantity surveyor do?

We look after the money side of construction: what a project should cost, whether claims match the work done, and where the budget stands as things change.

When should I bring in a QS?

The earlier the better, as design decisions have the biggest effect on cost. We can also step in part-way through to check a tender, assess claims or get a budget back on track.

Is it worth it for a smaller project?

Usually, yes. A detailed estimate on a renovation, new home or insurance repair often saves far more than it costs.

Do you work outside Christchurch?

Yes, across the South Island. Much of the work is done from drawings, and we visit site whenever the project needs it.
