About OGQS

Quantity surveying without the unnecessary complexity.

Owen-Goodall Quantity Surveyors (OGQS) provides independent quantity surveying and construction advisory services focused on making projects easier to understand, manage and deliver.

We work alongside our clients rather than simply producing numbers. That means understanding the project, identifying commercial risks early and providing straightforward advice when decisions need to be made.

From a renovation or residential build through to larger construction projects, our aim is simple: give you clear information so you can make informed decisions and keep your project moving.

Practical advice. Clear numbers. Accountable delivery.
