Not sure if you need a QS?

A few of the questions we hear most. If yours isn't here, just ask.

What does a quantity surveyor actually do?

A quantity surveyor looks after the money side of construction. We work out what a project should cost, check prices and claims against the work actually done, and keep track of changes along the way, so you always know where the budget stands.

When should I bring in a QS?

The earlier the better. Decisions made at feasibility and design stage have the biggest effect on cost. That said, we're often brought in part-way through a project to check a tender, assess claims and variations, or get a budget back under control.

Is a QS only worth it for big projects?

No. A detailed estimate on a renovation, a single new home or an insurance repair can save far more than it costs. We scale our involvement to suit the project, from a one-off estimate to ongoing support.

Do you work for builders as well as property owners?

Yes. We help builders and subcontractors with estimating, progress claims, variations and final accounts, and can step in as extra QS capacity when a team is stretched.

Do you work outside Christchurch?

Yes. We're based in Christchurch and work with clients across the South Island. Much of our work is done from drawings and project documents, and we visit site whenever the project needs it.

What do you need from me to get started?

Plans or drawings if you have them, a rough idea of scope, budget and timing, and anything you've already been quoted. If it's still early days, a conversation is a perfectly good place to start.

How are your fees worked out?

Fees depend on the size of the project and how involved you need us to be. Send through a short description and we'll provide a clear quote before any work starts.
